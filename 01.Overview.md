##1. Overview & Problem
The Problem
Employees frequently ask HR the same questions:

"How many leave days do I get?"
"What's the notice period?"
"Can I work from home every day?"
The answers to these questions are already in HR policy documents, but nobody wants to dig through them.

The Solution: HR Policy Assistant
The HR Policy Assistant is a chatbot that:

Answers employee questions by actually reading the company's real HR policy documents.
Quotes directly from the documents, ensuring accuracy — not by guessing.
